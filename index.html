Fibonacci pivots, in the group before the desk opens.

Every morning we publish today's Fibonacci levels from yesterday's high, low and close. One pivot, three resistance, three support. When price tags a level, the group hears that too.

Pivot(H + L + C) ÷ 3
R1P + 0.382 × (H − L)
S1P − 0.382 × (H − L)
R2P + 0.618 × (H − L)
S2P − 0.618 × (H − L)
R3P + (H − L)
S3P − (H − L)

Levels, not calls. Pivot points show where price may react. The decision stays with your client, so nothing crosses into signals.
